How San Diego Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Many mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, central air typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of the year.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected through a full season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast the moment something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.
