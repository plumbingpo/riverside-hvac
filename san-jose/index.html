How San Jose Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Many mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.
